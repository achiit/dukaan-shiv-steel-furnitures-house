Our story

A workshop first, a showroom second.

I started with a small welding set on Bayana Road. My elder brother stood next to me for the first orders, and my first almirah went to a neighbour three streets away. He still says it has not rusted.

Everything you see in the shop is cut, bent, welded and painted by our own people. I would rather lose an order than sell a thin sheet that dents in the first monsoon.

Come in, press the doors, try the locks, and tell us what you need. If we do not have your size, we will make it.

  • Thick steel sheet and proper welding, not thin tin
  • Powder-style finish in many colours, easy to wipe clean
  • Branded locks and handles, keys included
  • Custom sizes made on request
  • Delivery on the promised day, setup help included
